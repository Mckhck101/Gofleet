# Gofleet

Gofleet is an intelligent travel booking and transportation management platform designed to simplify mobility through digital technologies and artificial intelligence.

The platform allows users to search, compare, reserve and track transportation services including buses, trains and flights while benefiting from smart recommendations, digital ticketing and real-time travel information.

## Vision

To become the leading intelligent mobility platform in Central Africa by combining transportation services, artificial intelligence and digital travel experiences into a single ecosystem.
